# A — Workshop sheet

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: original Lantern Smithing screen design study, preview only, not production assets.
Create a polished desktop fantasy RPG UI mockup at a 16:10 composition representing a 1280 x 800 viewport. Center a complete usable menu on an almost-black abstract backdrop, with no gameplay scene or world art. Warm crafted fantasy: soot-charcoal opaque reading surfaces, forged iron structure, restrained worn brass joints, ivory text, one amber primary action, subtle tactile workmanship. Restrained ornament and exceptionally clear information hierarchy. Short Gothic heading, highly readable mixed-case sans-serif controls and numerals. Keep item and material illustrations original, painterly and compact, no oversized display art.
EXACT SAME SCREEN, CONTENT AND STATE ACROSS ALL DESIGN STUDIES:
Title "Smithing". Close X at top right.
Header shows "Level 6", "250 / 600 XP", and a small XP progress indicator filled about 42 percent. NO next-unlock information.
Two mode tabs: "Forge" selected, "Reclaim" inactive.
Exactly FOUR learned recipes, named "Sword", "Shield", "Guard Helm", "Weathered Mail". Weathered Mail is selected. No locked recipes, no Iron Broadsword, no unknown recipes, no talent tree.
Selected output is "Weathered Mail", with an original painted chainmail shirt icon drawn at compact inventory scale, roughly 96 x 144 pixels in the intended viewport, plus the property "+12 Armor". Its fixed craft cost is "4 Iron". Material availability is "8 available", with a clear quieter breakdown "Bag 3" and "Stash 5". No Wood requirement for this selected recipe. Selected craft reward "+3,000 Smithing XP". One enabled amber primary button "Forge". This is the idle, ready-to-forge state; a completed forge will take 2 seconds but no fake progress, countdown or success is displayed now.
Maintain the same content in all variants. No quantity selector, gold, wallet, shop, stat comparison, paper doll, character, lore, explanatory footer, invented feature, extra recipe, skill points, random quality, enchantment, percentages on equipment, labels like prototype/lab, or ornamental slogan. No private/licensed art, no Synty or Mixamo references, no external brands, no 3D game models. Original text-only concept.
Layout variable A: a strong traditional two-column workshop sheet, roughly 28 percent narrow recipe navigation on the LEFT and 72 percent selected-recipe detail on the RIGHT. Four quiet vertically stacked text-first recipe rows, selected row subtly warm. The right area groups small mail illustration beside its name and armor property, then clearly organized material requirement/availability and XP reward below; primary Forge button aligned at lower right in that detail area. The header and Forge/Reclaim tabs span the sheet. Thin vertical divider, no card grid. Concentrate detail at panel joints and keep the text field calm. Compact panel around 900 x 570 intended pixels with generous margins.
