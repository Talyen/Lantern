## Source and preparation

The five owner-supplied ZIPs contain numbered pieces of one XZ-compressed tar package, containing `B1_Adventurer_Rigged.glb`, README, material/outfit metadata and MakeHuman provenance/license records. Unchanged ZIPs, reassembled package and extracted sources remain under `.local/animation-packs/Protagonists/b1/`; the legacy archive is deduplicated only after its complete collection is verified at this canonical main location. No character models or embedded textures are tracked.

Clone animation sources into an owned task with `npm run agent:sources -- --sources animation-packs`, then run `npm run assets:export-character -- --player-only`. The wrapper first protects the complete supplied collection on main when recovering from an unambiguous retained task archive, then clones the required source directory into the task; differing archived sources require explicit reconciliation. See [source retention](DEVELOPMENT_REFERENCE.md#retention-and-disk-use). Prepared output stays under ignored `public/vendor/characters/b1/`. `--motions-only` retains the prepared authored model and refreshes its motions/catalog.

The supplied GLB already has a 52-bone humanoid rig, normalized skin weights, ten facial controls on the five face meshes, 471,292 triangles, 100 materials and 169 embedded images. It contains no animations. The three additional source ZIPs retain the editable high-detail Blender master separately from this reduced runtime model. `B1_Reusable_Animations.zip` retains seven skeletal actions, individual FBXs, manifests and retargeting/source tools. These excerpts are not certified seamless loops and do not contain the matching garment tracks; they do not replace the reviewed weapon profiles. Blender source is preferred for garment corrections and editable materials; FBX is useful for animation interchange. Neither is required to bind the existing Lantern motion library.

[Character preparation](../scripts/assets/characters/b1.py) keeps every outfit part and groups the three skinned lantern pieces as `character-lantern`. A non-rendered `lantern-socket` follows the authored belt hanger. [The shared personal light](../src/rendering/player-lantern.ts) toggles the supplied lantern with its light preference, preserves Golden emitter/bounce positions and avoids adding a second cage. Other lab rigs retain the existing separate-lantern path. B1 has no quiver in the supplied outfit.

## Motion compatibility

Canonical bone names retain the existing gameplay attachment rules and complete finger mapping. B1-specific `equipment-socket-Hand_L/R` frames reconcile her wrist/palm axes with the reviewed shared item grips; other rigs continue using their hand bones. The sockets include Blender's preserved glTF joint-axis offset, and keep weapon calibration beside the character recipe rather than changing gameplay item definitions. B1's A-pose bone directions are aligned to each T-pose source before applying world-space motion deltas. This is opt-in for B1; existing Goblin/Skeleton bakes retain their behavior. Simply matching names or copying Erika-baked tracks folds B1's carrying arms inward and misplaces grips.

All current player equipment profiles, directional locomotion, gathering, dodge, reactions and abilities are independently baked at 60 fps using [the motion manifest](../assets/motion-profiles.json). Original sources, contact/release markers and gameplay timing remain unchanged. The supplied default subtle-mouth expression and other facial controls remain authored. The nine historical/study selections are also independently prepared; study motions remain outside the gameplay catalog.

## Acceptance

Prepared-art/reference validation passes for all three playable rigs. All 57 B1 clips (48 catalog, nine study) bind to the prepared skeleton with 52 mapped bones at 60 fps. All 169 embedded image payloads match the source exactly; the ten facial controls and authored defaults remain on all five face meshes, and mesh positions differ by at most 0.00000085 m of export rounding. Native WebGPU review exercised normal Homestead movement, Axe attack and dodge, then inspected Axe, Bow, Staff, Sword/Shield and running poses on B1. The initial Staff wrist-frame mismatch was corrected at the equipment sockets. The authored lantern toggles independently of the outfit. No runtime errors were observed in those interactions. Checks establish bindings and source/reference validity, not cross-platform visual or performance acceptance. Use one owned normal-settings gameplay preview; expand motion inspection for a demonstrated rig or garment defect. Keep actual captures privately with the task evidence.

The supplied README reports skirt intersection in its raw-skeletal jog and recommends matching garment correctives or cloth. Its cloak/skirt are skinned, with no runtime cloth simulation. Motion binding alone does not establish garment clearance. The model is substantial and has no final material/texture-atlas optimization. Its richer authored appearance was reviewed at gameplay scale; the setup retains source textures and detail. No benchmark or cross-platform performance certification is implied by importing it.
